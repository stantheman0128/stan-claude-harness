---
name: grilling
description: 拷問既有的計畫或設計，逐一追問到雙方有共識。主動使用本 skill 當使用者已經有一份 plan/design/架構草稿、想在動手前被狠狠質問、走過設計樹每個分支逐一定案決策（每題附建議答案、一次只問一題），或講「grill 我」「拷問我」「狠狠質問這個計畫」「動手前先壓力測試」「stress-test this plan」「poke holes in my design」。不要看到計畫就直接照做或直接開工，先用本 skill 把決策追問清楚。使用者還沒有計畫、需求講不清楚（缺 who、why 或成功標準），或講「先訪談我」「interview me」時，走本 skill 的「還沒有計畫」段，先挖出真正想要什麼。
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on each question before continuing. Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering.

If a *fact* can be found by exploring the codebase, look it up rather than asking me. The *decisions*, though, are mine — put each one to me and wait for my answer.

Do not enact the plan until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.

## 還沒有計畫

If I only have a vague ask (who it is for, why now, or what counts as done is missing), interview me about intent before any plan exists:

- Attach your best guess to every question, with the reasoning behind it. Now and then guess in a direction you expect me to push back on.
- "Whatever you think", "sounds good" and silence are not a yes. Ask me to pick.
- Stop when you can predict my answers to your next three questions. Then restate the goal, who it is for, what done looks like, and one line on what is out of scope, and wait for my confirmation.
