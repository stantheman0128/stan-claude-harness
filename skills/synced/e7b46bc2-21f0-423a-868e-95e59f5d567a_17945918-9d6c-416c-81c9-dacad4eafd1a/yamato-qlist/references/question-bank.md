# Q List Question Bank — distilled from four CDIB deals

This is the concrete companion to `SKILL.md`. It is a library of real question patterns from four past Q lists, templatized for reuse. **It is not a list to send as-is.** Pull the relevant patterns, re-anchor every `[placeholder]` to the deal at hand's actual numbers and names, match the language to the recipient, and sequence into waves/channels per the rules in `SKILL.md`.

Tags on each pattern: **deal** of origin · **fit** (which business-model archetype it's mandatory for; "any" = universal) · **channel** (W written / V verbal-leaning) · **wave** (1 business / 2 governance / 3 docs).

- Case study w/ third-party validation: "客戶 Case Study：[良率提升 / 換模時間縮短 % / 節能] 之具體數據與第三方驗證。" · Moldintel · industrial · W · 1

## Per-deal profiles
Compact context so the reasoning behind each list is recoverable.

**圖靈 (Turing) — 2025-01-09 · Series A.** Blockchain certificate / verification platform built on IOTA; revenue across SaaS + project-based + value-added/API; markets TW/JP/Others. List language: **English**, 17 Qs. Distinctive moves: valuation reconstruction from share count × price (→ implied post-money, confirm ESOP); customer-vs-account definition; platform infra fee economics (IOTA storage + verification charging); **quoting a weak retention number (55%) and asking the cause**; growth-momentum decomposition ("2-3x from where, by market"); project costing & outsourcing %.

**Jitera — 2026-01-30 · priced round.** AI agent *context* platform for software development (competes with Cursor); strong Japan logo base (six of Japan's top-10 by market cap). List language: **English**, 11 Qs — tight and SaaS-metric-dense. Distinctive moves: pricing-anchored seat/customer growth; ARPA evolution; MRR New/Expansion/Churn; monthly retention; **specific competitor-M&A awareness** (Cursor acquired Graphite & Supermaven); **platform risk** (if OpenAI offers long-context); confirmed pre-money + who has committed + amount to date; comps list.

**JTCG / 詹姆斯科技 (James Tech) — 2026-04-07 · Seed.** Zendesk **reseller** + Raccoon AI customer-service **SaaS** (hybrid); Subscription/Advertising/Consulting revenue lines. List language: **Chinese**, 29 Qs — the deepest on financials and governance. Distinctive moves: revenue-definition questions; GM gap (38.24% reported vs ~60% claimed) **and** the bridge to 62.32%; cost-line-from-zero (Zendesk & Professional Service = 0 in 2024); base-business profitability (was the Zendesk agency profitable pre-AI); freemium conversion; usage-priced (conversation-based) counts; reseller geographic rights & local competitors; AR/AP days; **cap-table-vs-SHA ESOP mismatch**; **CB-on-balance-sheet** reconciliation + why CB; **redemption clause** (8% compound if 2027 revenue < NT$100M) rationale; **share transfers to founders** (119,996 shares) reason & price; reference-customer interview request (OmniChat).

**Moldintel — 2026-06-01 · Pre-A (Yamato's upgraded version).** Injection-molding process AI; strategic investors 震雄機械 (molding-machine OEM) and 台達電 / 台達資本 (power & automation). List language: **Chinese**, 30 Qs across 8 categories. Distinctive moves (the maturity benchmark): reading the audited report line-by-line and anchoring numbers (授權 −14% / 商品銷售 +103%; 銷售費用 +148%; balance-sheet 其他金融資產 +12.6M / 預付投資款 +11.1M); revenue-quality decomposition; CAC/payback/CAC:LTV by channel; **cap-table-vs-變更登記表 discrepancy** (益創二創投 0 vs 548,393 shares); **strategic-investor side-letter** probe; co-founder FTE; patents; cross-customer data-training rights; weighted pipeline; third-party-validated case study; org chart + key-person CVs incl. CFO; explicit exit-route question.
